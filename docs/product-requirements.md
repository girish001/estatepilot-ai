# EstatePilot AI — Product Requirements

## Goal
Convert property enquiries into qualified conversations and confirmed site visits using a transparent, friendly AI calling assistant.

## Lead intake
Capture source, name, phone, consent evidence, preferred language, property category, preferred locations, size/type preferences, purchase purpose, and customer-stated timeline. Never collect a total budget or affordability ceiling.

## Property inventory
Support plots, independent houses, flats, and villas. Store verified location, category, unit/area, available area, approved rate per square foot (or approved unit rate where square-foot pricing is not applicable), amenities, availability status, approved disclosures, brochure/media links, and last verification timestamp.

## Pricing policy
1. Quote only the approved rate per square foot from active verified inventory.
2. Do not ask for or store a customer's total budget.
3. Do not negotiate, offer discounts, or claim a final deal price.
4. For negotiation requests, explain that a sales executive can discuss it one-to-one during a property/site visit.
5. If asked for total cost or additional charges, share only approved factual information and clarify that final details are confirmed by the sales team. Never imply the per-square-foot rate includes unverified charges.

## Conversation
Be warm, respectful, concise, and transparent that the caller is an AI assistant. Ask one question at a time. Confirm key details. Match only against verified inventory. Offer WhatsApp details only with appropriate consent.

## Follow-up and site visits
Schedule callbacks only for a date/time the customer agrees to. Respect local calling windows, retry limits, opt-outs, and do-not-contact status. Site visits have requested, confirmed, reminded, completed, cancelled, and no-show states. If a visit is missed, ask once whether the customer wants to reschedule; do not continue if they decline.

## Human handoff
Escalate unknown property questions, legal/approval questions, complaints, negotiation, payment discussions, and explicit requests for a human.

## Reporting
Export lead profile, enquiry source, consent metadata, requirements, matched properties, approved quoted rate, call outcomes, callback schedule, site-visit status, next action, and timestamps. Protect personal data and restrict report access to authorized users.
